Meet the maker

Art, nature, energy, and a love for creating.

Old Soul Gem was born from the belief that the objects we surround ourselves with can hold meaning, tell stories, and bring beauty into everyday life.

Created by Alejandra—an Usui Reiki Master, artist, and maker—the shop brings together handmade crystal jewelry, sculptural creations, and carefully chosen vintage finds.

The process is intuitive and personal: natural materials are allowed to keep their variation, handmade details keep the creator's touch, and each piece waits for the person who connects with it.

A look inside the process

Guided by the material. Finished by intuition.

From opening a new parcel of rough opal to finding the right charm for a bracelet, the materials often reveal what they want to become.

  1. Begin with a stone, natural texture, vintage find, or feeling.
  2. Explore color, movement, shape, and the energy of the piece.
  3. Finish by hand, then release it to find the right person.
