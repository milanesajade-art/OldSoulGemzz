The spirit behind the shop

Designed around the soul of the stone.

Old Soul Gem begins where perfectly matched jewelry ends—with natural variation, asymmetry, color, and the feeling that a piece could belong to only one person.

Each concept starts with a gemstone or an emotion, then grows into a setting with its own rhythm. The result can be delicate, celestial, botanical, or unapologetically bold.

Created in San Antonio and developed as a living collection, every piece is documented with its own name, materials, and story.
